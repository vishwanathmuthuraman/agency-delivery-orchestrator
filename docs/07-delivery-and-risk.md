# 07 — Technical limitations and research risks

The application is not implemented. Synthetic scenarios describe required behavior; they do not establish autonomous project management, customer adoption or measurable savings.

| Limitation | Consequence | Verification or disclosure required |
|---|---|---|
| Incomplete dependency capture | A changed claim may leave affected outputs unidentified. | Expose uncertainty and evaluate traversal separately from model extraction. |
| Stale or ambiguous approval | An old decision may be applied to new scope or content. | Bind authority to exact revisions, purpose and current sources. |
| Uncertain external outcome | A lost response may hide a successful write. | Reconcile before retry and preserve unresolved outcomes. |
| Completion versus observation | Sending a request does not prove delivery or acceptance. | Require destination evidence and separate review/closure states. |
| Partial creative support | An editing packet is not a rendered video or audio correction. | Label supported transformations and require returned artifacts. |
| Client isolation | A guessed ID or cached fragment may cross tenant boundaries. | Test storage, retrieval, cache, model context, reports and errors. |
| Synthetic reviewer behavior | Predetermined review schedules can favor a strategy. | Use a common reviewer contract and report simulated work separately. |
| Supplied answers | Replaying reviewed impacts can resemble autonomous understanding. | Freeze predictions before feedback; separate semantic and control experiments. |
| Scope expansion | A broad lifecycle may mask an unfinished core. | Keep public claims aligned with verified [milestones](03-build-plan.md). |

Money, authority, identity, state transitions and idempotency belong to deterministic rules. Optional model proposals remain untrusted inputs. Commercial approval, creative acceptance, dispatch authorization and billing readiness are distinct decisions.

Mock connectors do not establish real platform guarantees. An offline result does not demonstrate legal compliance, generalized staff management, live client satisfaction or return on investment. Existing tools overlap with parts of the proposed workflow; no claim of exclusive novelty is made.

See [evaluation](04-evaluation.md), [release gates](05-definition-of-done.md) and [publication rules](08-publication.md). Public fixtures remain fictional; real client records, connector credentials and private commercial plans are excluded.
